---
title: Credits
description: How 1billion counts a send, the free credits of each day, and what happens when they end.
---

Each workspace has a balance of credits. A mail to one recipient costs 1,000 credits.

## The credits of the day

Each day, at 00:00 in the time zone of the workspace (Brasília by default), the balance goes back up to 100,000 credits: 100 mails a day, every day, for free. A new workspace gets the credits of its first day at once. The credits of the day do not carry over.

## What costs what

| Result | Credits |
|---|---|
| A mail that the server of the recipient accepted | 1,000 |
| An address that does not exist (`5.1.x`, or no such domain) | 2,000 |
| A mail blocked for our reputation (`5.7.x`), or one we could not send | 0, we pay it back |
| A recipient on the suppression list | 0 |
| A recipient that fails a check before the send (`422`) | 0 |
| A webhook event that your app answered with 2xx | 100 (a failed try costs nothing) |
| An address validation (`POST /validate`) | 100 |
| The first domain of a workspace | 0 |
| Each domain after the first, one time | 1,000,000 |

The console shows each line for the last 30 days, on the Credits page.

## When the credits end

A send with less than 1,000 credits for each recipient gets `429`, and nothing is sent. The body has `retry_after` (the seconds until the credits of the next day) and `buy_url`:

```json
{
  "error": "not enough credits for 2 recipients: the credits of the day come back at 00:00 in America/Sao_Paulo, or buy a pack",
  "retry_after": 41000,
  "buy_url": "https://app.1billion.clowk.in/usage"
}
```

## Credit packs

A pack is 1 billion credits for R$ 59.99, paid once, with no monthly fee. The credits of a pack never expire, and a workspace with them sends 5 mails a second. Buy as many packs as you need: 2 packs are 2 billion credits.

The credits of the day do not add to a pack: when the balance falls under 100,000, it goes back up to 100,000 at midnight.
